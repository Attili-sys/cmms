## Reusable instruction for a reverse-engineering agent

Analyze only the local reference material and permitted public links in this archive. For each selected target, report: personas; entry points; record/relationship concepts; screen regions; key controls; observed states and transitions; documented validation/permissions; empty/error/loading/offline states actually evidenced; and unresolved questions. Distinguish documentation observations, screenshot observations, marketing claims and your design proposals. Cite source IDs and image hashes. Produce an independent Atlas specification rather than copying Facilio artwork, prose or code. Inspect Atlas’s actual version before claiming a feature is missing. Do not authenticate, execute integration/API/CLI actions, submit forms, scan services or bypass excluded resources.

## Suggested passes

1. **Experience foundation:** targets 1–4, 11–13. Build one coherent navigation and record/action vocabulary.
2. **Enterprise operations:** targets 5–10 and 14–17. Specify obligations, evidence and commercial handoffs.
3. **Platform and assistance:** targets 18–22. Generalize proven needs, then add context-aware extensions and AI.
