## Scope summary

The form builder provides typed controls, lookups, sections, subforms, uploads and signatures. Field properties and conditional rules control visibility, defaults, requirements and editing, with role-specific template access. This suggests a reusable schema/form layer rather than separate hardcoded forms for each business process.
