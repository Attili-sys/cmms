# Vendors, contractual service and SLA controls

Research date: **2026-10-17**. Public documentation supports the flows below; marketing claims are identified separately. Atlas was not inspected, so these are candidates for assessment rather than established missing features.

## Vendor identity and portal experience

[Working with Vendors](https://facilio.com/help/docs/user-guide/facilio-modules/vendor-management/working-with-vendors/) separates vendor organizations from their contacts and connects vendor status, insurance, portal access and request assignment. [Managing Vendors and Vendor Contacts](https://facilio.com/help/docs/user-guide/facilio-modules/vendor-management/working-with-vendors/managing-vendors-and-vendor-contacts/) documents primary contacts, related contact lists, editing and activation/deactivation. Insurance validity can affect vendor status automatically. The interaction to study is a managed operational relationship, not merely a supplier address book.

[Enabling Portal Access](https://facilio.com/help/docs/user-guide/facilio-modules/vendor-management/working-with-vendors/enabling-portal-access/) shows an administrator selecting a contact and role before enabling access. The documented invitation leads to password creation and activation. This is research evidence only; no invitation or account operation was performed. [Managing Service Request Lifecycle](https://facilio.com/help/docs/service-request-management/managing-service-request-lifecycle/) describes vendors receiving assigned work and reflecting progress and completion back to tenant-facing requests. The [portals/workcenters overview](https://facilio.com/help/docs/admin-guide/General/portals-and-workcenters/overview/) supports distinct persona layouts, authorization settings and separate desktop/mobile presentations.

## SLA policy model

[Managing SLA Policies](https://facilio.com/help/docs/admin-guide/automation-plus/managing-sla-policies/working-with-slas/managing-sla-Policies/) documents policy scope by module, site and criteria. Commitments define due intervals under additional conditions, using fixed durations or duration fields and calendar/business-hour modes. Escalations attach actions to breach timing and subsequent levels. Crucially, escalation intervals are relative to each level rather than necessarily absolute from the first breach. Policies can be ranked and toggled. These details warrant their own configuration UX and explainable timing model; a single “due date” field would not reproduce the demonstrated behavior.

## Contracts: evidence strength is mixed

The [vendor product page](https://facilio.com/product/vendor-management-software/) presents insurance/compliance imagery, renewal/amendment tracking and vendor performance/SLA dashboards. These are public product claims and visual references, not a complete documented contract workflow. The [features catalog](https://facilio.com/features/) lists purchase, lease, rental and warranty contract concepts, importing PO lines into a contract, and applying agreed SLAs. [Dated release notes](https://facilio.com/release-notes/) provide additional evidence for contract-linked work-order widgets and access/scoping refinements in 2026. They establish ongoing capability development without revealing every intermediate screen or validation rule.

## Hypothetical Atlas opportunities and unresolved questions

Assess organization/contact separation, vendor self-service, document expiry, access provisioning, contract coverage and SLA audit trails in the actual Atlas deployment. Possible premium features include contextual compliance warnings, vendor scorecards and contract-to-work-order traceability. Keep policy selection and timer calculations explainable to operators. The public record does not fully establish contract amendment approval, renewal negotiation, legal document versioning, commercial entitlements or which noncompliant-vendor actions are hard blocks versus warnings. A standalone guessed vendor-portal help URL was inaccessible in web research; do not infer undocumented UI from that URL.
