## Scope summary

Work orders combine execution and financial context with tasks, time entries, history and related records. The illustrated sequence includes optional approval, assignment, starting work, completing tasks, resolution, closure and reopening. Study which actions appear at each stage and where downtime and supporting evidence are recorded.
