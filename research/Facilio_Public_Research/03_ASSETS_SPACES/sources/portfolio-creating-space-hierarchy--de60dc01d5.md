## Scope summary

The location model supports sites, buildings, floors, spaces and nested spaces. Spaces may also be associated directly with sites or buildings. The guide covers timezone/boundary and occupancy information, floor plans and a visual space builder. Model optional hierarchy levels rather than enforcing a single rigid nesting chain.
