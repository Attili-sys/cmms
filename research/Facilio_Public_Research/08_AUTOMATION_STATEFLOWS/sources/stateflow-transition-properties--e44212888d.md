## Scope summary

Transitions can be manual or automatically triggered and may combine permissions, geography, QR verification, forms, validations, confirmations and follow-up actions. QR-required transitions have documented mobile-specific constraints. This is a reference for controlled business transitions, not simply editable status fields.
