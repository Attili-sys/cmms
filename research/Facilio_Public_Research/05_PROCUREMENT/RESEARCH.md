# Procurement, quotes and financial handoffs

Research date: **2026-10-17**. This research summarizes public Facilio workflows. Atlas was not inspected, and proposed upgrades are hypotheses. Public marketing is distinguished from step-by-step documentation.

## Documented purchasing flow

The [procurement overview](https://facilio.com/help/docs/procurement-guide/overview/) frames a linked process: purchase request, sourcing when needed, vendor selection, purchase order and receipt. An existing supplier can support a more direct PR-to-PO route. The [stage index](https://facilio.com/help/docs/procurement-guide/managing-procurement-process/illustrating-procurement-stages/) connects the individual instructions for requests, RFQs, vendor quotes, POs, receivables and terms.

[Requesting for Quotation](https://facilio.com/help/docs/procurement-guide/managing-procurement-process/illustrating-procurement-stages/requesting-for-quotation/) is the highest-value screen sequence. Users create and publish an RFQ, receive proposals, close submissions, compare vendors and award the work. Open and closed bids differ in when quotes are visible. The guide requires at least one quote before submissions can close. Assessment can award an entire order or different line items to different vendors. Award summaries can switch between vendor and line-item perspectives, and awarded vendor records expose PO creation. Study this transition from operational list to comparison workspace and onward to the next commercial object.

[Generating Purchase Orders](https://facilio.com/help/docs/procurement-guide/managing-procurement-process/illustrating-procurement-stages/generating-purchase-orders/) shows a dedicated PO list and creation screen after vendor selection. [Viewing Procurement Details](https://facilio.com/help/docs/procurement-guide/viewing-procurement-details/) shows summary, vendors, notes/information, related records and history. RFQs distinguish all invited vendors from awarded vendors. Notes include a private/public distinction for portal communication, an important enterprise collaboration pattern.

## Service quotations are another linked flow

[Working with Quotations](https://facilio.com/help/docs/user-guide/facilio-modules/quote/managing-quote/working-with-quotations/) documents generating a quote from work-order financials, receiving vendor pricing, converting to a client/tenant quote with markup, managing terms and retaining version history. Draft editing and sent-quote revision are distinct actions. [Creating Quotation](https://facilio.com/help/docs/user-guide/facilio-modules/quote/managing-quote/creating-quotation/) supplies the commercial form reference: line items, tax, markup, dates, addresses, discounts, additional charges and signature.

## Invoice evidence and integration boundaries

The [procurement product page](https://facilio.com/product/procurement-automation-software/) claims partial receipt handling, quantity validation, invoice-to-receipt/PO matching, invoice approval and payment visibility. Its imagery is useful, but this is weaker evidence than a complete invoice user-guide flow. The [Xero integration guide](https://facilio.com/help/docs/integrations/xero-integration-for-facilio/) documents contact synchronization followed by quote/invoice/PO/bill synchronization, backed by installed fields, buttons, functions and schedulers. Its stated synchronization cadence is inconsistent, so confirm rather than copy it.

## Hypothetical Atlas opportunities

Audit current purchasing and costing first. Potential upgrades are linked commercial records, multi-vendor comparison, line-level awards, immutable quote revisions and visible financial handoffs. Avoid claiming verified three-way matching, payment execution, general-ledger behavior or complete contract accounting from these sources. Procurement UX depends on permissions, currencies, tax rules and ERP ownership decisions that public screenshots do not settle.
