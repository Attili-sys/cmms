### 1.2 Facilio's Core Operating Thread

As synthesized in [`01_PRODUCT_MODULES/RESEARCH.md`](file:///Users/attiliab/Documents/GitHub/cmms/research/Facilio_Public_Research/01_PRODUCT_MODULES/RESEARCH.md), Facilio's distinguishing value is its **connected end-to-end operating chain**:

$$\text{Where work happens (Portfolio)} \longrightarrow \text{What equipment serves it (Asset 360)} \longrightarrow \text{Who is responsible (Team / Vendor)} \longrightarrow \text{What commitment applies (SLA / Calendar)} \longrightarrow \text{What evidence proves completion (Stateflow / Inspection)} \longrightarrow \text{What it costs (RFQ / PO / Quote)}$$

## 3. Deep-Dive Technical Analysis Across Facilio's 12 Domains

### 3.1 Maintenance Execution, Job Plans & Resource Planner (`02_WORK_ORDERS_MAINTENANCE`)

- **Facilio Reference ([`02_WORK_ORDERS_MAINTENANCE/RESEARCH.md`](file:///Users/attiliab/Documents/GitHub/cmms/research/Facilio_Public_Research/02_WORK_ORDERS_MAINTENANCE/RESEARCH.md))**:
  1. **Four-Stage Lifecycle (`F-bb26eed29a`)**: Separates **Authorization** (approval before work starts) $\rightarrow$ **Execution** (assigned technician starts work, logs labor/parts, completes mandatory tasks/photos) $\rightarrow$ **Resolution** (technician marks work resolved; asset downtime stops) $\rightarrow$ **Closure** (supervisor/requester verifies and closes; closed work can be reopened with audit reason).
  2. **Job Plans vs. PM Planner (`Configuring Planned Maintenance` & `Configuring Job Plan`)**: Facilio separates the **Job Plan** (a versioned, reusable procedure specifying sections, tasks, conditional mandatory photos, planned labor hours, planned parts, and planned tools/services) from the **PM Planner** (which binds recurrence triggers, execution windows, skipped cycles, assets/spaces, and technician assignments to a Job Plan).
  3. **Resource Planner (`F-b33bd8ad03`)**: A dispatch workspace pairing an unassigned work queue with a technician/team timeline calendar supporting drag-and-drop scheduling and date-granularity switching.
- **Current AJ CAFM Architecture & Gaps**:
  - [`WorkOrder`](file:///Users/attiliab/Documents/GitHub/cmms/atlas-cmms/src/models/work_orders.py) and [`PreventiveMaintenance`](file:///Users/attiliab/Documents/GitHub/cmms/atlas-cmms/src/models/pm_schedule.py) share [`WorkOrderBase`](file:///Users/attiliab/Documents/GitHub/cmms/atlas-cmms/src/models/work_orders.py), while procedures are represented by [`Checklist`](file:///Users/attiliab/Documents/GitHub/cmms/atlas-cmms/src/models/tasks.py).
  - **What to Enhance in AJ CAFM**:
    - **Separate `RESOLVED` (Technician Done) from `COMPLETE/CLOSED` (Verified)**: Or enforce a `verification_status` (`NONE`, `PENDING_QA`, `VERIFIED`, `REJECTED`) on [`WorkOrder`](file:///Users/attiliab/Documents/GitHub/cmms/atlas-cmms/src/models/work_orders.py) so technicians can complete field work while supervisors hold final closure authority.
    - **Upgrade `Checklist` into a Full `JobPlan`**: Allow a [`Checklist`](file:///Users/attiliab/Documents/GitHub/cmms/atlas-cmms/src/models/tasks.py) to include estimated labor hours, required craft/role, and planned spare parts (`PartQuantity` templates) so attaching a Checklist/Job Plan to a PM or Work Order automatically populates planned tasks, parts, and estimated duration.
    - **Build the Dispatch Resource Planner View**: Add a `Resource Planner` tab in [`frontend/src/content/own/WorkOrders/`](file:///Users/attiliab/Documents/GitHub/cmms/frontend/src/content/own/WorkOrders) showing unassigned open work orders on the left and a technician/team swimlane timeline on the right.

### 3.5 Stateflows vs. Event Workflows (`08_AUTOMATION_STATEFLOWS`)

- **Facilio Reference ([`08_AUTOMATION_STATEFLOWS/RESEARCH.md`](file:///Users/attiliab/Documents/GitHub/cmms/research/Facilio_Public_Research/08_AUTOMATION_STATEFLOWS/RESEARCH.md))**:
  - Facilio makes a brilliant architectural distinction that solves the exact workflow confusion seen in SentrixFM:
    1. **Stateflows (`F-dc49c9c20b`, `F-e44212888d`)**: Govern **human/timed state transitions** of a record (`Open -> Assigned -> In Progress -> On Hold -> Resolved -> Closed`). Each transition defines *who* can click the button (`Role`), *what preconditions* apply (e.g., mandatory mobile QR scan of the asset, geolocation check, or all tasks completed), *what mini-form* pops up (e.g., `Hold Reason` or `Resolution Notes`), and *what post-actions* fire.
    2. **Event Workflows (`Configuring Workflow Parameters`)**: Govern **automated background reactions** to record events (`On Create`, `On Field Change`, `On Threshold Breach`) that update fields, send notifications, or spawn related records without changing the lifecycle graph.
- **Current AJ CAFM Architecture & Gaps**:
  - [`Workflow`](file:///Users/attiliab/Documents/GitHub/cmms/atlas-cmms/src/models/workflows.py) models Event Workflows (`WFMainCondition`, `WorkflowCondition`, `WorkflowAction`), but lacks a runtime execution service, and `WorkOrder` transitions are currently unguarded status field edits.
  - **What to Enhance in AJ CAFM**:
    1. **Build `atlas-cmms/src/services/workflow_engine.py` (`P0`)**: Execute stored `Workflow` rules on entity create/update events.
    2. **Build `atlas-cmms/src/services/stateflow_service.py` (`P0/P1`)**: Define an explicit transition policy table (or configurable `StateflowPolicy` per module/category) that returns the list of **valid next transition buttons** (`GET /work-orders/{id}/transitions`) and validates preconditions (`require_tasks_complete`, `require_signature`, `require_qr_scan`, `require_reason`) when `POST /work-orders/{id}/transition` is called.

### 3.7 Record-Scoped AI Co-Pilots & Form Assistance on Canvas (`12_AI`)

- **Facilio Reference ([`12_AI/RESEARCH.md`](file:///Users/attiliab/Documents/GitHub/cmms/research/Facilio_Public_Research/12_AI/RESEARCH.md))**:
  - Rather than offering an ungrounded generic chat box, Facilio splits AI into **4 high-trust interaction patterns**:
    1. **Technician Record Co-Pilot (`F-8e0ab2ec31`)**: Scoped strictly to the currently open Work Order / Asset. Summarizes past repairs, answers questions from attached asset manuals, and drafts resolution notes with an explicit **"Approve / Apply Change"** preview card.
    2. **Form Assistant (`F-4e163df3d6`)**: Takes a pasted email, voice note, or PDF invoice and populates standard form fields in place with **visual confidence indicators**, keeping the human form as the final gate before saving.
    3. **Photo Validator**: Compares before/after task photos and presents an evidence completeness score to the reviewing supervisor.
    4. **FM Portfolio Co-Pilot**: Answers cross-site operational queries (capped at 50 records per action with mandatory human confirmation before bulk writes).
- **Why AJ CAFM Has a Structural Advantage Here**:
  - Because AJ CAFM runs natively on **Canvas (`aj-cafm`)** with `ajnkit-core`, we can implement **Record-Scoped Technician Co-Pilot** and **Form Assistant** directly inside `atlas-cmms/` using Canvas's native authentication, database session scoping, and AI SDK—enforcing that every AI-suggested update goes through the exact same FastAPI validation and audit history as a human edit.
